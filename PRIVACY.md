# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 18, 2026_

Figures is a spreadsheet app for macOS and iOS. This policy describes what the app does with your information.

## What Figures collects

Nothing. Figures does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal information or usage data.
It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it does not make network requests.

## Your files

Spreadsheets you open or create stay on your device, or wherever you choose to save them, such as iCloud Drive
or another file provider. Figures reads and writes only the files you open or save with it. Any syncing is
handled by Apple or your file provider under their own privacy policies.

## Children

Figures does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published in this repository with a new date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at https://github.com/tariknaeem/Figures/issues.
