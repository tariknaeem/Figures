Figures does not read every part of an Excel workbook. When you save, anything it does not support, such as
charts, images, comments, pivot tables, data validation, macros and some formatting, is **not kept**. Keep a
backup of any workbook before editing and saving it in Figures. The author is not responsible for lost or
